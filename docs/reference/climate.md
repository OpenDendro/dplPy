# Climate response

Correlation of a tree-ring chronology with monthly and seasonal climate
(SEASCORR; Meko, Touchan & Anchukaitis 2011), and reconstruction of climate from
a tree-ring network by orthogonal spatial / principal-components regression (OSR;
Cook, Briffa & Jones 1994). See the
[Climate–growth response](../guide/seascorr.md) guide for worked examples.
