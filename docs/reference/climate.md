# Climate response

Correlation of a tree-ring chronology with monthly and seasonal climate
(SEASCORR; Meko, Touchan & Anchukaitis 2011), the **stability** of that
correlation through time by a moving-window analysis with Monte-Carlo
significance (`moving_corr`), superposed epoch analysis of event responses
(`sea`), and reconstruction of climate from a tree-ring network by orthogonal
spatial / principal-components regression (OSR; Cook, Briffa & Jones 1994). See
the [Climate–growth response](../guide/seascorr.md),
[Moving correlation](../guide/moving-correlation.md) and
[Superposed epoch analysis](../guide/superposed-epoch.md) guides for worked
examples, and [Reconstruction validation](../background/reconstruction-validation.md)
for how `pcreg` validates a reconstruction and what each statistic in
`result.stats` means.
