    Climate response¶

    Correlation of a tree-ring chronology with monthly and seasonal climate (SEASCORR; Meko, Touchan & Anchukaitis 2011), and reconstruction of climate from a tree-ring network by orthogonal spatial / principal-components regression (OSR; Cook, Briffa & Jones 1994). See the Climate–growth response guide for worked examples, and Reconstruction validation for how pcreg validates a reconstruction and what each statistic in result.stats means.

    seascorr¶

    Seasonal correlation of a tree-ring chronology with monthly climate.

    A faithful port of SEASCORR (Meko, Touchan & Anchukaitis 2011). The chronology is correlated (simple Pearson) with a primary climate variable and (partial, controlling for the primary) with a secondary variable, across season-lengths x 14 ending months. Significance is assessed by exact spectral simulation of the chronology.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    crn Series or DataFrame

    Year-indexed chronology (standard or residual). If a DataFrame, pass column= to choose the index series.

    		 required
    primary DataFrame or Series

    One monthly climate variable each, in any of three shapes: wide (year index/column + 12 month columns), tidy (year, month, value), or a monthly datetime/period-indexed series.

    		 required
    secondary DataFrame or Series

    One monthly climate variable each, in any of three shapes: wide (year index/column + 12 month columns), tidy (year, month, value), or a monthly datetime/period-indexed series.

    		 required
    end_month int

    Last month of the growth year (1=Jan ... 12=Dec).

    		 9
    season_lengths sequence of int

    Season lengths in months; the first must be 1.

    		 (1, 3, 6, 12)
    primary_agg (sum, mean)

    How each variable is aggregated over a season (sum for totals such as precipitation, mean for temperature).

    		 "sum"
    secondary_agg (sum, mean)

    How each variable is aggregated over a season (sum for totals such as precipitation, mean for temperature).

    		 "sum"
    nsim int

    Number of exact simulations; must be >= 1000.

    		 1000
    period None, (first, last), or ((e0, e1), (l0, l1))

    Analysis window. A single pair sets the window; a pair of pairs sets an early and a late window and triggers the stability test.

    		 None
    seed int or None

    Seed for the surrogate random generator (reproducible significance).

    		 None
    column str or None

    Chronology column to use when crn is a multi-column DataFrame.

    		 None
    plot bool

    Draw the seasonal bar chart (Figure 3 of the paper).

    		 True

    Returns:

    Type Description
    SeascorrResult

    SeascorrResult¶

    Result of :func:seascorr.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    table DataFrame

    One row per season: season_length, end_month, label, r_primary, p_primary, r_partial, p_partial, r_clim (primary-vs-secondary), and the two-sided significance flags sig_05 / sig_01 for each correlation.
    period tuple(int, int)

    First and last year of the analysis window.
    nsim int

    Number of exact simulations used.
    end_month, season_lengths analysis settings.
    highest DataFrame

    Strongest primary-variable season per season-length.
    acf ndarray

    Lag 1-5 autocorrelation of the chronology over the window.
    stability DataFrame or None

    Two-period difference-of-correlation test, when two windows were given.

    pcreg¶

    Orthogonal spatial regression / PC-regression climate reconstruction.

    A faithful port of Ed Cook's OSR FORTRAN (Cook, Briffa & Jones 1994). The predictors are screened against the target over the calibration window, reduced to principal components, and the target is regressed on the PCs that pass a t-test; the fit is back-transformed to per-predictor weights, a reconstruction is produced over the full predictor period, and (if a validation window is given) it is validated against the withheld data.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    predictors DataFrame or Series

    Year-indexed tree-ring predictors (e.g. the output of :func:read_crn). Sample-depth companion columns are dropped automatically.

    		 required
    target Series or 1-column DataFrame

    Year-indexed climate target to reconstruct. Pre-seasonalize it yourself (e.g. a March-May PDSI mean) before calling.

    		 required
    calibration (first, last)

    Calibration window, inclusive.

    		 required
    validation (first, last) or None

    Validation window, inclusive. When None, only the calibration fit and the reconstruction are returned (no validation statistics).

    		 None
    screen_alpha float

    Screening significance level. Use 1.0 with screen_tails=2 to force every predictor into the PCA ("normal response function").

    		 0.1
    screen_tails (1, 2)

    One- or two-tailed screening test.

    		 1
    screen_sign ('+', '-', None)

    Required correlation sign for a one-tailed test.

    		 '+'
    min_coverage int or None

    Minimum overlapping years to admit a predictor. Defaults to the full calibration length (every predictor must cover the whole window), which is what the fixture uses.

    		 None
    pc_criterion (pvp, kaiser, first_m, cum_pct, all)

    PC truncation rule (Cook's eigenvalue-cutoff options 3, 1, 0, 2, 4).

    		 'pvp'
    n_pc int

    Number of PCs for pc_criterion='first_m'.

    		 12
    ev_cutoff float

    Rescaled-eigenvalue cutoff for pc_criterion='kaiser'.

    		 1.0
    cum_pct float

    Cumulative-variance percent for pc_criterion='cum_pct'.

    		 90.0
    order_method tstat

    PC entry/stop rule. Phase 1 implements the t-statistic rule.

    		 'tstat'
    t_cutoff float

    |t| threshold for order_method='tstat'.

    		 1.0
    prewhiten bool

    Prewhiten predictors and target with a best-fit AR model before screening (reuses :func:dplpy.autoreg.ar_func). The AR order is chosen by AIC up to ar_max.

    		 False
    ar_max int

    Maximum AR order when prewhiten=True.

    		 10
    ar_method (burg, yw, ols)

    AR estimator. 'burg' (maximum entropy) matches Cook's memcof.

    		 'burg'
    lag None, 11-char mask string, or sequence of int

    Lead/lag the predictors before screening (Cook's lagit). Each predictor is expanded into one column per offset: offset d uses the predictor at year Y + d to predict year Y (d < 0 = a lag on earlier ring years, d > 0 = a lead). Give the offsets directly (e.g. (0, 1) or (-1, 0, 1)) or as an 11-char mask over positions -5..+5 (e.g. "00000110000" = offsets 0 and +1). The same mask is applied to every predictor.

    		 None
    cv None or int

    Cross-validate by refitting the whole pipeline on held-out folds of the calibration period. cv=1 is leave-one-out; cv=k leaves out contiguous k-year blocks. Results land in stats under the cv_ keys (cv_r, cv_RE, cv_CE; cv_RE and cv_CE coincide over the calibration window). None skips cross-validation.

    		 None
    first_differences bool

    Also report validation statistics on the 1st-differenced series (the FORTRAN's second validation pass), under _diff keys in stats.

    		 True
    plot bool

    Draw the reconstruction/validation figure.

    		 False

    Returns:

    Type Description
    PcregResult
    Notes

    A single target, PC-regression core with optional AR prewhitening, lead/lag predictor masks, 1st-difference validation, and leave-k-out cross-validation. Deferred to a later phase (and documented in dev/pcreg_DECISIONS.md): canonical regression, robust / biweight correlations, and the Southern-Hemisphere auto-shift.

    PcregResult¶

    Result of :func:pcreg.

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    reconstruction Series

    Year-indexed reconstruction of the target over the full predictor period, in the target's own units (variance rescaled to the calibration mean and SD).
    calibration, validation tuple(int, int) or None

    The calibration and validation windows (first, last).
    predictors_screened list

    Column labels retained by the screening step, in column order.
    screen_info DataFrame

    Per-predictor r, t and p from screening.
    n_pc int

    Number of principal components retained.
    pcs_selected list of int

    1-based PC numbers entered in the regression, in entry order.
    cal_r2 float

    Calibration R-squared.
    stats dict

    Validation statistics on the undifferenced data, the same set on the 1st-differenced data under _diff keys, and -- when cv was requested -- the cross-validated skill under cv_ keys. The panel mirrors Cook's verify: RE, CE; Pearson r (r_p); spearman and kendall rank correlations (_p); the robust biweight midcorrelation (robust_p); the sign_pos/sign_neg sign test (sign_p); the product-means test pm_diff (pm_t, pm_p); and the equality-of-means test eqmeans_diff (eqmeans_t, eqmeans_p). Significance comes from SciPy's exact distributions; the correlation p-values are one-sided (positive skill expected) and the sign test is an exact binomial. robust is the modern biweight midcorrelation (equivalent to astropy's), which replaces Cook's older sum/difference robcor.
    cv_reconstruction Series or None

    The pooled held-out cross-validation prediction per calibration year (target units), or None when cv was not requested.
    dropped_coverage list

    Predictor columns excluded before screening for insufficient calibration coverage (with lead/lag, because the lagged window runs past their data). Reported as a warning at fit time; kept here for inspection.
    weights Series

    Back-transformed weight per screened predictor.
    eigenvalues ndarray

    Original eigenvalues of the screened-predictor correlation matrix (descending).
    settings dict

    The resolved analysis settings.
